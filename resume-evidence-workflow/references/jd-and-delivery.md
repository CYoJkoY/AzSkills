# JD Alignment and Delivery

Read this reference before mapping a concrete role, arranging sections, running the
visible alignment review, or choosing a delivery route. One execution plan governs
the work: this Skill. Only the artifact engine and the editor surface may come from
another owner.

## Keep the requirement intact

- Keep the complete original job description through final verification. A pasted
  summary, a recruiter paraphrase, or a keyword list is not the requirement.
- Preserve attachments, language, channel instructions, and stated constraints
  separately from the prose.
- Record what is missing. Do not infer a must-have from a job title or from a
  colleague's description of the role.
- Re-read the original after every targeted revision. A keyword list loses the
  relationship between responsibilities, level, and context.

## Build the routing profile

Extract and record:

```text
market and hiring conventions
resume language and locale
role family and specialization
industry and company type
candidate stage: internship, graduate, career switch, experienced, leadership
application channel: ATS upload, recruiter, referral, portfolio, public profile
requested artifact: text, Markdown, DOCX, editable HTML, PDF
source-layout preservation needs
single-application or reusable-base scope
explicit presentation and retention instructions
```

The profile decides language, section shape, artifact, and file naming. It never
decides which claims exist.

## Map requirements to evidence

Build the matrix before writing prose:

| Requirement | Weight | Evidence | Status | Intended treatment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| The specific responsibility or capability | must, preferred, or signal | Bank entry, project module, or accepted expression | direct, adjacent, weak, or absent | Emphasis, compression, position, or gap |

Status meanings:

- **Direct:** confirmed evidence shows the capability at comparable scope and
  context.
- **Adjacent:** the same underlying capability appears in a different context and
  transfers with an honest bridge.
- **Weak:** the connection is plausible but thin, incomplete, or unconfirmed.
- **Absent:** no supplied or confirmed evidence supports the requirement.

- Write one row per distinct requirement. Do not collapse a must-have into a
  preferred signal because the evidence is weak.
- State intended emphasis, compression, movement, and gaps before drafting.
- Do not rewrite first and rationalize the match afterward.
- A must-have with absent evidence is a reported gap. It does not authorize an
  invented claim, and it does not automatically block a usable resume.
- Give an overall suitability judgment or apply recommendation only when the user
  asks. Never assign a default fit score or percentage.

## Choose the owning method

- This Skill owns career facts, requirement mapping, selection, resume wording, and
  delivery decisions.
- Compose with `ui-design` only when the editor chrome itself needs construction,
  refinement, accessibility work, or a visual review. The resume paper keeps its
  own design.
- Use the available document tool that owns a format this Skill does not render,
  such as DOCX or PDF conversion, and state which engine produced the file.
- Consult an external specialist only for a concrete missing method. Inspect its
  claims, license, dependencies, and data handling first. Installation or connection
  requires the user's choice, and the specialist method runs inside this plan
  without importing a second approval flow.
- Record the route: owning Skill and mode, artifact engine, reasons, limitations,
  and user decisions on meaningful conflicts. This keeps later variants
  reproducible.

## Preserve section and relationship integrity

Classify every selected entry before arranging sections: formal employment,
internship, part-time or freelance work, project, campus activity, volunteering,
independent operation, or another relationship type.

- If formal employment exists, give it an appropriate section. Do not place formal
  employees inside an internship section, and do not let internships define the
  whole structure.
- When both exist, keep them in separate identifiable sections. Formal employment
  normally appears first for an experienced candidate unless relevance or an
  explicit preference supports another order.
- When the candidate has only internships, use an internship section without
  creating an empty or misleading employment section.
- For an experienced candidate, retain an early internship only when it still adds
  useful evidence. Recommend omission when appropriate, and obtain approval before
  deleting it.
- Do not mix employment, internships, projects, freelance work, campus
  organizations, volunteering, personal content, competitions, coursework, or
  informal collaborations inside a vague combined section.
- Preserve the exact relationship at entry level: intern, employee, part-time
  contributor, freelancer, project lead, volunteer, creator, or participant. A
  section title may not upgrade the relationship.

Choose sections from the actual evidence rather than from a template. Clear Chinese
labels include:

- `工作经历`
- `实习经历`
- `项目经历`
- `兼职与自由职业经历`
- `校园经历`
- `志愿服务`
- `个人项目`

Equivalent labels in another language must preserve the same boundaries.

Prioritize the relationship type in the section name. Add a role-specific function
only when every entry in that section shares both the relationship type and a
coherent function, for example `品牌与市场工作经历` or `内容运营实习经历`. Use the
simpler label when functions vary, evidence is limited, or a narrower label would
distort the entries.

Avoid vague headings such as `专业经历`, `相关经历`, `综合经历`, or `市场经历`
when they hide whether entries are employment, internships, projects, or
independent activity. Similar subject matter is not enough reason to merge
different relationship types, and a tailored heading may never imply employment,
scale, or ownership the evidence does not support.

Confirm that a recruiter can identify within seconds which entries were formal
employment, internships, or non-employment experience; whether formal work receives
visibility appropriate to the career stage; whether ordering fits both the stage and
the requirement; and whether any heading obscures the relationship type.

## Keep V0 and plan readable space

- Save the pre-revision content as V0 before editing. V0 covers the summary, every
  user-supplied section, and the bullets being changed, not only the lines that
  later differ.
- Allocate readable space by requirement priority before drafting: rank sections,
  estimate the line budget, then write to it.
- Render a first content pass before the alignment review, especially for HTML, and
  inspect density, wrapping, and date collisions.
- Reconstruct the professional summary for this requirement from verified evidence.
  A general base resume keeps a general summary.

## Write natively for the target language

- For Chinese, concise context and meaningful work-focus labels are allowed; a
  typical bullet carries context, action, and a verifiable result without becoming a
  compressed slogan.
- For English, use standalone action-led bullets and integrate necessary context
  inside the sentence. Avoid literal translation, noun piles, and comma-spliced
  Chinese rhythm.
- Neither language accepts machine-translated jargon, decorative adjectives, or
  responsibilities the user did not hold.
- Localize dates, degree names, organization suffixes, locations, currency, and
  punctuation conventions.
- Keep the user's accepted spelling of names and organizations.

## Set approval boundaries

- Ask before materially deleting or merging a user-supplied experience, project,
  overview, bullet, education item, or section. Irrelevance is a reason to
  recommend removal, not authorization to remove it.
- Reuse an approval only for the scope it covered. A company-specific cut does not
  become a permanent default.
- Minor copyedits, authorized repairs, and reversible formatting do not need a new
  approval.
- If the user requests a presentation choice that differs from the evidence record,
  carry it through and keep it separated from the bank. State a material explanation
  risk once, then continue.

## Run the visible alignment review

Run this review after every targeted content revision and before delivery:

1. Reread the complete original job description. Identify the responsibilities,
   expected outputs, and capabilities that drive this revision.
2. Revisit the evidence index and the relevant sections. Search beyond the current
   resume. Check ownership, dates, metric scope, and confirmed decisions.
3. Compare V0, the current draft, and the requirement. Reconstruct the narrative
   where needed rather than substituting keywords. Review selection, emphasis,
   summary, language, concision, and readable space.
4. Repair material issues within the existing authorization. Propose any material
   deletion or merger for a user decision.
5. Show a concise table of meaningful changes:

| Before | After | JD requirement addressed | Evidence and reason |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Summarize the actual V0 expression | Show the revised expression | Identify the specific responsibility or capability | Cite the evidence module and explain the change |

- Use actual text, not invented examples. Group minor copyedits instead of narrating
  each punctuation change.
- Include important retained content when its relevance needs explanation, and list
  unresolved evidence gaps plainly.
- If no material change was needed, say so with the review basis.
- Conclude with what was repaired, what remains unresolved, and whether the artifact
  is ready. Missing evidence is a gap to report; it neither blocks a usable resume
  nor justifies an unrequested fit judgment.

## Final market and artifact checks

Workflow:

- The final resume follows the latest instructions and covers the intended
  priorities.
- No information was added that the user neither supplied nor requested.
- Presentation choices that differ from the evidence record stay separate from it.
- Internal labels, confidence scores, placeholders, and advisor notes do not ship.

Market and language:

- Conventions fit the target country, language, industry, seniority, and company
  type.
- An English resume reads as a native recruiting document and a Chinese resume as a
  native Chinese document; neither is a literal translation of the other.
- Sensitive personal information and photo conventions match the market and the
  user's preference.

Selection and control:

- Material deletions and mergers were approved, and the route and any overridden
  rule are recorded.
- Confirmed summary and target-position preferences were reused; unresolved choices
  were clarified.
- The target-position decision fits the file's scope. A general base resume does not
  receive a narrow target role by default.
- The user's section choices override template defaults.
- Relationship types remain distinguishable and formal work receives visibility
  appropriate to the career stage.
- Section order fits the career stage and the requirement, and no name misstates
  employment type, role, or ownership.

Content:

- V0 is preserved and the alignment review ran after targeted revisions.
- Material changes are shown with their requirement and evidence.
- Writing is native to the language, space was planned, and the first content render
  was inspected.

Artifact:

- The delivered format is the one the application requires. Editable HTML follows
  [editable-html.md](editable-html.md).
- The source layout is preserved when requested.
- Page count, clipping, overlap, whitespace, readability, alignment, links, and print
  behavior were checked.
- The file opens independently without missing local assets.
- The file name identifies the person and, when useful, the employer.

## Hand off

Deliver the finished resume, not only a critique or a diff. State briefly:

- which owner, mode, and artifact engine produced the file, and why;
- the most important positioning choices;
- unresolved evidence gaps that could matter in an interview;
- how to make future variants from the saved evidence bank.

Keep maintained sources and derived artifacts distinguishable in the handoff. Do not
claim ATS parsing benefits, recruiter outcomes, or conversions that were not
verified.

## Public examples

If an example, case library, screenshot, or public demonstration is created, use
wholly fictional identity and career data. Change names, organizations, dates,
project details, and metrics together. Removing only a name leaves the case
re-identifiable.

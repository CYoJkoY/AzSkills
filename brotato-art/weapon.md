# Weapon Module

Use this module for melee weapons, firearms, magic weapons, sci-fi weapons, improvised weapons, and other attack equipment.

## Construction Order

1. Define the weapon's recognizable profile.
2. Establish its primary mass and orientation.
3. Add grip, attachment, or mounting geometry only when needed.
4. Add one dominant material/color group.
5. Add minimal functional details.
6. Apply bold contour and flat shading.

## Weapon Identity

The silhouette matters more than realistic engineering. A sword should read as a sword, a gun as a gun, a wand as a wand, and a strange fantasy weapon through its distinctive profile. Exaggeration is preferred over technical accuracy when it improves gameplay readability.

## Detail Budget

Keep the number of major internal structures low. Prefer two or three large readable components over many small parts. Remove screws, seams, vents, grooves, cables, and mechanical micro-details unless they are essential to identity.

## Materials

Represent materials through simple color grouping rather than physically accurate rendering. Metal, wood, stone, bone, crystal, energy, and organic materials should each be recognizable from shape and palette, not PBR highlights.

## Effects

If a weapon includes an energy glow, flame, projectile, magical aura, or impact indicator, keep the effect subordinate to the weapon silhouette unless the effect itself is the subject.

## Prompt Skeleton

> [Weapon], immediately recognizable chunky silhouette, [orientation], [major components], limited color palette, bold black outer contour, clean flat color blocks, one or two hard-edged cartoon shadow shapes, minimal functional details, simplified 2D indie game weapon sprite, high thumbnail readability, transparent background.

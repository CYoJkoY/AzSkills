# Enemy Module

Use this module for ordinary enemies, elite enemies, bosses, mutants, aliens, monsters, mechanical opponents, and other hostile entities.

## Core Rule

Enemies can be more aggressive, strange, grotesque, or intimidating than protagonists, but they must remain inside the same simplified 2D cartoon rendering system.

## Construction Order

1. Design the silhouette and movement identity.
2. Establish the head/body mass or equivalent focal mass.
3. Add one dominant hostile feature.
4. Add secondary appendages only when useful.
5. Apply limited palette and simple shadows.
6. Validate readability at gameplay scale.

## Monster Language

Use exaggerated mouths, eyes, horns, tentacles, claws, spikes, shells, mechanical parts, or other motifs as chunky graphic shapes. Cthulhu-like and mutant concepts should be communicated through silhouette and a few distinctive motifs rather than realistic body horror.

## Gameplay Readability

An enemy should be distinguishable quickly by silhouette, dominant color, and one signature feature. Avoid large areas of tiny details that disappear during gameplay.
